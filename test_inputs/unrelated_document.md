# Electronics Lab Equipment Checkout Procedure

## Overview

This procedure governs how students and staff borrow shared equipment from the Electronics
Teaching Lab, including oscilloscopes, function generators, soldering stations, and multimeters.
It exists to keep the lab's limited inventory available and in good working order for everyone.

## Purpose

The purpose of this procedure is to establish a consistent process for checking equipment in and
out of the lab, assigning responsibility for borrowed items, and recovering equipment that is
overdue or damaged.

## Scope

This procedure applies to all registered students, teaching assistants, and staff who use
equipment housed in the Electronics Teaching Lab, room 114B.

## Procedure

### Checking equipment out

1. Every borrower must present a valid student or staff ID at the front desk before any
   equipment is released.
2. The front desk staff must record the equipment's asset tag, the borrower's name, and the
   expected return date in the checkout log before handing over any item.
3. A borrower must not check out more than two major instruments (oscilloscope or function
   generator) at the same time without written approval from the lab manager.
4. Equipment must be visually inspected for damage by both the borrower and the front desk
   staff before it leaves the lab, and any existing damage must be noted on the checkout log.

### Using equipment

5. Borrowed equipment must remain within the building at all times and may not be taken off
   campus.
6. Borrowers must power down and safely disconnect all equipment before transporting it within
   the building.
7. Any malfunction discovered during use must be reported to lab staff the same day; the
   borrower must not attempt to open or repair the unit themselves.

### Returning equipment

8. Equipment must be returned no later than 7 calendar days after checkout, or sooner if a
   reservation from another borrower is on file.
9. The borrower must be present when the returned equipment is inspected, and the front desk
   staff must confirm the asset tag matches the checkout log before closing out the loan.
10. A borrower who returns equipment more than 3 days late may have checkout privileges
    suspended for the remainder of the term.

## Policy Compliance

Lab staff will spot-check the checkout log weekly against physical inventory. Repeated failure
to follow this procedure may be referred to the department's lab safety committee.
