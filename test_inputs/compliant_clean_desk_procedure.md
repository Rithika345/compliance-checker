# Office Clean Desk Procedure

## Overview

This procedure describes how our team keeps sensitive and confidential information secure at
our desks and workstations. It applies to everyone who works from a desk in our office space,
including full-time staff, contractors, and interns.

## Procedure

### Securing your desk

At the end of each day, and whenever you expect to be away from your desk for an extended
period, all sensitive or confidential material in hardcopy or electronic form must be secured
rather than left out in the open. Any restricted or sensitive documents must be removed from the
desk surface and locked in a drawer when the desk is unoccupied and at the end of the work day.
File cabinets that hold restricted or sensitive information must be kept closed and locked
whenever they are not actively in use or attended.

Keys that provide access to restricted or sensitive information must never be left sitting at an
unattended desk; take them with you or store them somewhere secure.

### Securing your workstation

Computer workstations must be locked whenever the workspace is unoccupied, even for a short
break. At the end of the work day, workstations must be shut completely down rather than left
sleeping or logged in. Laptops must either be secured with a locking cable at the desk or locked
away in a drawer, and the same goes for other portable computing devices such as tablets.

Never leave a password written on a sticky note posted on or under a computer, or written down
anywhere else within easy reach of the desk.

### Handling printed and disposed material

Printouts containing restricted or sensitive information should be picked up from the printer
immediately, not left sitting in the output tray. When restricted or sensitive documents are no
longer needed, they should be shredded in the office shredder bins or placed in the locked
confidential disposal bins rather than a regular wastebasket.
